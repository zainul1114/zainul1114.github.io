About Azilehub Academy


Azilehub Academy provides deep technical learning pathways focused on the intersection of robust DevOps pipelines and scalable MLOps environments. In an era where infrastructure automation meets artificial intelligence, engineers need holistic mastery over container runtimes, monitoring frameworks, and accelerated cluster schedulers.

Our curriculum is built on real-world engineering paradigms. Whether you are establishing local and cloud container architectures via Docker and Portainer, setting up metric-driven observability with Prometheus and Grafana, or scaling deep learning models across Slurm and PBS Pro high-performance computing clusters, our tutorials offer step-by-step clarity.

We empower practitioners to architect resilient relational databases with PostgreSQL, build vector-search capabilities with Qdrant, and serve local LLMs using high-throughput execution engines like vLLM and Ollama.
